Frequently Asked Questions

Is this password generator free?

Yes. The generator is designed to work without an account.

Are generated passwords sent to a server?

No. Password generation is performed locally in your browser.

What makes a password strong?

Length, randomness and uniqueness are key factors. A long random password is generally much harder to guess than a short predictable one.

Should I reuse the same password?

Avoid reusing passwords between important accounts. A password manager can help you store unique passwords safely.
